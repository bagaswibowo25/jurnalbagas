When asking the owner a decision question, first say in plain Indonesian what exists now and what the term refers to, then ask, with concrete options describing what changes on the site.

**Why:** in 1994-erp the owner answered a scope question with "saya nggak ngerti ini maksud dari standar itu apa?" because the question used an internal label without context.

**How to apply:** each question = one short context sentence (current state) + concrete options. Related: [[sdd-v2]], [[ui-review-workflow]].
